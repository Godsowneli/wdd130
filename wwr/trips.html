Find Your Adventure

Beginner River Adventure

Difficulty: Easy

This introductory trip is designed for guests who are new to rafting. A guide explains basic paddling, safety procedures, and teamwork before the group heads onto the water.

Suggested duration: 2 hours

Whitewater Challenge

Difficulty: Moderate

Work together as a team and follow your guide through livelier sections of river. This trip is for guests seeking a more energetic experience who are comfortable following paddling instructions.

Suggested duration: 3 hours

Scenic Group Expedition

Difficulty: Easy to moderate

Enjoy time on the river with friends or family, take in the surrounding scenery, and practise paddling together. The guide adjusts instructions to the group's experience and conditions.

Suggested duration: 2.5 hours
